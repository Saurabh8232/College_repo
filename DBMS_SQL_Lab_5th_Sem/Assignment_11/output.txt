
Assignment 11: Other Kinds of Joins

Task 1: LEFT JOIN, club on the left

mysql> SELECT c.club_name, m.member_name
    -> FROM club c
    -> LEFT JOIN member m ON c.club_id = m.club_id
    -> ORDER BY c.club_name, m.member_name;
